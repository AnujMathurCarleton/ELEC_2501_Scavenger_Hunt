Electric current is defined as the rate at which charge flows past a point in a circuit, given by the relationship I = Q / t, where Q is the charge in coulombs and t is the time in seconds.

  1. Using this relationship, determine the current I that flows when a total charge of 10 coulombs passes a given point over a period of 10 seconds.
  2. Determine the total charge Q that has flowed if a steady current of 2 amperes takes 3 seconds to deliver it.
  3. Determine how much time t it takes for a charge of 50 coulombs to flow past a point when the current is held at 10 amperes.
  4. The standard metric prefixes, listed here from smallest to largest, are: femto (f), pico (p), nano (n), micro (µ), milli (m), the base unit (1), kilo (k), mega (M), giga (G), and tera (T). Using this ordering, evaluate the expression −log₁₀(1p) — that is, find the power of ten that the pico prefix represents, expressed as a positive number.
